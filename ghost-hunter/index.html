      Brodie the Ghost Hunter

      Hollow Creek High

      Prom night, 1987. Three spirits never left the school, and every clock stopped at 11:47.

      Difficulty

      Head scan by Lee Perry-Smith (CC BY 3.0). Character rig from Mixamo. Night sky HDRI from Poly Haven (CC0). Textures from the three.js examples. Generated art made with Higgsfield.

      How to play

      1. Each spirit is anchored by a relic hidden somewhere in the school. Find it.
      2. Your UV beam only drives a spirit away. Carry its relic and the beam burns it until it is at rest.
      3. UV light reveals handprints, footprints and writing that lead to the relics. Notes help too.
      4. Running is loud and your torch gives you away. Crouch in the dark to stay unseen.
      5. Hide in lockers and hold your breath when something comes close.
      6. Put all three to rest, then get back to the car before the clock strikes.
